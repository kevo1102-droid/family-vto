# For Casey — a few questions

Hey love. I'm putting together something for us — a "where are we going as a family" doc. Same format businesses use to align on where they're headed, but for us. I've got my side started. Rather than make you fill in a form, I'd rather just hear your answers to a few questions in whatever way feels easy — text, voice memo, over coffee.

No wrong answers. Short is fine. Don't overthink.

---

## Who we are

**1. What are 3 things you'd want people to know about our family — the stuff that makes us *us*?**

**2. What's a behavior you've seen in another family that made you think "not us"?** (Sometimes what we're not tells us what we are.)

**3. When you're proudest of us as a family, what are we usually doing?**

---

## Why we're doing all this

**4. Why do you think our family exists? Like — bigger picture, what are we here to do together?**

**5. What would make you feel like we "made it" as a family — not financially, but in terms of how we lived?**

---

## Where we're going

**6. When you picture our life in 10 years, what does a normal Tuesday look like? Where are we? Who's with us? What are we doing?**

**7. Three years from now — what's different about our life that you'd be excited about? Get specific if you can.** (House, money, kids, travel, hobbies, work, health — whatever hits.)

**8. What's one thing you want us to be doing this year that we're not doing yet?**

---

## Right now

**9. What are the 1–3 things you think we should be really focused on over the next 90 days?**

**10. What's something that's been on your mind about us / the house / life that we haven't really talked about?** (No wrong answers. Even small stuff.)

**11. Is there anything you feel like you're carrying alone that you want us to be carrying together?**

---

## Fun ones

**12. What's one tradition you want us to have — or keep having?**

**13. If we could add one thing to our life this year that we've been putting off, what would you pick?**

---

Take your time. Send back however's easiest.

— K
